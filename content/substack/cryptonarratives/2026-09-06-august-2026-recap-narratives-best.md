## The debasement trade

The most important theme of the month for BTC has been the debasement trade. BTC has been trading in a very tight range around $64K from early July to mid-August, and the complete lack of volatility was suggesting that the next BTC move could be explosive. It was logical to be expecting that move to be to the upside too because BTC was basically unable to dump on bad news, despite Saylor selling and despite the Coldcard hack, there were clearly a lot of buyers sub $60k that did show up in June/July.

The real catalyst for the move happened on August 19, when Bessent announced that he would double the buybacks on the long-end of the curve (= buying back 30y US bonds), which is a liquidity increasing mechanism. Gold immediately reacted by pumping +4% on the back of the “debasement trade” news, and BTC started pushing a few hours later. It squeezed hard from $64k to $70k in a day, and then didn’t find any rest for 2 days and it kept squeezing, to as high as almost $80k. Since then, BTC has mostly been flat in a range close to $78k-$80k, and if I had to bet I would say that the next move is to the upside.

Altcoins have naturally pumped with BTC too, they had 3 very strong days of pump from August 19 to 22, and it culminated with a strong squeeze on August 22 (rotation from BTC into alts after a local top on BTC) that then lead to a moderate flush which stopped the momentum on most alts. Many CEX altcoins have been bleeding for 10 days after that, and the real mania happened on-chain.

## On-chain mania on Robinhood chain (PONS, AI, the FOMO app)

The main narrative among altcoins in August has clearly been the on-chain mania on Robinhood chain. The chain has attracted a ton of attention, volume, and activity during the second half of August, and there have been a few winners that stood out:

- **$PONS** is the main meme launchpad on Robinhood, and it had an absolutely meteoric rise, as it went up more than 10x to $0.5 during the last week of August. It’s been generating more fees than Pumpfun on a daily basis, and a huge chunk of this revenue goes directly into buying back the token $PONS.

The mindshare around Bonk guy has drastically increased, and he’s still shilling USELESS as his main conviction play, which is why USELESS performed very well too.

## Mid-August bounce for AI stocks

July has been a massive month of deleveraging on the whole AI infrastructure trade, with sell-offs of -43% top to bottom for **$MU** and -59% for **$SNDK** among memory stocks for example. The whole dump culminated on July 29, which turned out to be when Leopold Aschenbrenner’s fund got “liquidated” at the bottom, before a strong bounce on July 30. After that first initial mean reversion impulse, most AI stocks have been consolidating in early August, but it was pretty clear that the next move would likely be to the upside, because the Situational Awareness fund collapse was too much of a signal for market participants to want to get exposed to AI stocks again. The move up happened in mid-August, and it was particularly strong for memory (+20% for MU, +50% for SNDK), as well as neoclouds (+50% for NBIS). After that, most stocks of the infrastructure trade have been chopping again if not outright bleeding, but it seems clear to me that the bottom of the trade is in and we still need to digest all of these moves before legging up again.

## The scam coins of August:

The scam coin category remained vibrant in August, with the main examples being **$TUT, $AKE** and **$BTW**:

- **$TUT** is a BSC memecoin that was trading around $10M mcap for several months in 2026, and that started moving up slowly in July, and then it accelerated big time in August, with roughly a 20x squeeze in 1 week, to $330M mcap highs. It then collapsed -90% in 5 days.
- **$AKE** had a 20x pump in July, and then it spent 2 weeks being almost flat at $0.004, which usually suggests on a scam coin that there are no organic sellers, so the price is likely to pump next. It’s what happened in mid August with a 4 squeeze.
- **$BTW** was launched in June and it always had a scammy price action. After trading almost flat during the month of July, $BTW had a 12x squeeze in August, to $7.5bn FDV at the top. It dumped down to $4bn FDV, and is again flat around there.

Other scam coins pumps in August include **$CYS** (+530%), **$SKYAI** (+460%), **$TAC** (+370%), **$BTR** (+580%)… all of which fully retraced their move up.

Once PUMP managed to break its $3bn FDV level, the rate of change increased and it suddenly pumped +85% in 4 days to $5.5bn FDV.

## What else happened in August?

- **$STRC**, the main preferred stock of Strategy, opened the month sub $90, and it has been slowly grinding upwards to go as high as $98. Saylor has been focused on trying to get it back to par, and he’s been selling MSTR shares for cash to increase his USD reserves and also to buy some STRC shares back on the market. By doing this, Strategy is progressively degrading its bitcoin-per-share ratio, which means that the market can “force Saylor’s hand” by not pushing STRC to $100 again. MSTR has been outperforming BTC in August though, and the current mNAV of the company is 1.15x. Saylor started buying BTC again in late August (funded by BTC sales).
- **$CHIP** is the token of [USD.AI](http://USD.AI), which is a stablecoin backed by GPUs. It is a high quality project with huge backers, and it had an extremely strong pump after its TGE, because it squeeze from $0.4 to $1.4 in 3 days. From there, it’s been bleeding for 3-4 months, it has fully retraced its pump and it went down -85%. But this is typically the type of coin that you want to buy once it’s forgotten and trading at depressed prices. It bottomed at almost $0.2 in August and then has been up only since then. Here’s [a long bullpost for CHIP](https://x.com/zeroxkyle/status/2094765093879980071) if you’re interested to know more.
- **$XRP** has been extraordinarily strong during the BTC pump from August 18 to August 22, as it squeezed from $1 to $1.7. This is a massive move for such a large cap, and it can be explained by the fact that XRP was probably shorted by a lot of people who were expecting it to break the $1 support, which ended up not happening despite a full week of trying. I do strongly believe that XRPBTC is now topped again for a long time, which implies that XRP could be a good hedge short to hold.
- **$KAITO** has been inexplicably strong in July, as it pumped almost 3x in a month to $1.37. It seems to have a been a crime driven move in the sense that it was inorganic and probably used to squeeze shorts, so it wasn’t surprising to see KAITO dump -75% in August.
- The **$TRUMP** memecoin had a massive squeeze on August 22, it went up 2x in a day before retracing -40%. From bottom to top, this corresponds to a +160% short squeeze, but I think it will remain a local top for a long time if not forever.
