# MARKET UPDATE: Hot Payrolls, Hotter Memecoins, And Se From FOMO Talks The Future Of Trading

**1000x** · 2026-09-04 · 1:06:07 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QVDm--18QBs)

## Thesis

Attention is now the tradable primitive across both crypto and equities. Social
trading platforms make attention legible, so the winning trade is to own the
leaders capturing it — not the laggards — into year-end.

## Macro setup

- Hot payrolls, unemployment 4.1%. Strong data has traders selling gold and
  pricing a higher odds of a **September Fed hike**.
- Bessent's claim that Treasury can spend up to $1T defending the long end puts
  Treasury at odds with the Fed. Avi reads this as **damaged Fed credibility**,
  which is why hot data isn't derailing risk assets.
- Crypto is bid for genuinely distinct reasons for the first time in a while:
  debasement trade (BTC, ZEC), social trading (memecoins), RWA/tokenization
  (Robinhood chain).
- Perception is diverging from data: a Pew poll shows Americans souring on the
  economy (~12% drop in "good/excellent") while the economy is rip-roaring.
  Drivers are the Iran war, gas prices, and AI job-loss fear. Avi: watch
  perception over the numbers.
- **Risk to crypto:** a returning broad-based AI trade. A narrow AI rally
  (Intel, SanDisk, Nvidia) can coexist with a crypto rally; a broad one
  (SK Hynix doubling, Korea going nuts) pulls attention and capital away.

## Interview: Se, co-founder of FOMO

**"Everything is attention."** Se argues capital markets are fragmented buckets
of attention, and crypto traders have an edge reading them — he went all-in on
HOOD off on-chain revenue data before equity markets noticed, then watched it
rip ~18%.

**Why FOMO couldn't exist two years ago:** insufficient cross-chain bridge
liquidity, slow relays, RPCs that couldn't take retail load, and no trusted
embedded wallets (Privy). Team came from a perps DEX (~90% market share, ~$1B
revenue in ~18 months) and concluded perps are unshowable to a normal person —
memes are legible, but were hard to trade. Moonshot proved the demand.

**Why social:** without a social layer, a new user buys one coin, wins, then
churns or blows up because they have no idea what's next. The social graph
supplies theses, track records, and time horizons. Trading is inherently
social — people buy Tesla because a friend they respect did.

**Creator rewards:** $4M paid out in 2 weeks; top earner ~$300k. The pitch is
that a good analyst monetizes orders of magnitude better than YouTube/Substack
without a paid course. Long-term vision: **"a thousand Roaring Kittys"** —
extend the graph to tokenized equities.

**The AI argument for capital-as-truth:** as AI commoditizes content, attention
stops being scarce. The new source of truth becomes *who is staking real dollars
publicly and being right over time*. That yields the highest-intent social graph
ever built — users already have funds on-platform.

**Drawbacks Se acknowledges:** front-running public sells ("it went up in
public, so it should go down in public"), influence-driven pumps, and time-horizon
mismatch between follower and followed. Only shipped mitigation so far is
**average hold time**. Ideas floated: buy-size-weighted reputation scores,
paid discussion groups. Positioning is deliberately **not the house** — not the
launchpad, AMM, or order book — so user longevity is aligned with the platform.

**Traction signals:** referred users fell from ~60% to ~25% (more organic
discovery). Growth in Nov/Jan was spiky 10x-over-two-days; the last 60–90 days
has been gradual 5–10%/day, which Se reads as durable rather than flash-in-pan.
Many users are net-new investors — for some this is the first thing they've ever
bought. Se trades on an anonymous alt account (~#200 on the leaderboard, up
~$400k) to avoid creating narratives.

## Avi's counterweight

- **94.4% of people have lost money trading on FOMO.** Avi attributes this to
  memecoins being gambling, not to the app.
- Memecoins are the only market that can price attention fast: an AMC tweet took
  a coin from $100k to $100M market cap in five hours. Equities can't; prediction
  markets barely can.
- **MemeFi** = tying a memecoin to an underlying stock (e.g. 20% of fees buy the
  equity), enabled by RWA tokenization on Robinhood chain. More real than past
  memecoins, but still a lottery ticket — "the memecoin version of a DAT," and
  nearly all DATs except MicroStrategy have failed badly.
- The market now rewards **real revenue** (Hyperliquid, Lighter, pump.fun) over
  2021-style "what could happen" narratives.

## Positioning and calls

- **"Beat the BART":** the Bart pattern is pump → chop → crash. Breaking through
  **80K BTC** without the down leg would mark a regime change; opens 100K and
  possible all-time highs by year-end. Constructive but explicitly *not* all-in.
- **Buy leaders, not laggards.** Chain abstraction killed the old beta trade
  (Uniswap rallies → Trader Joe clone rallies). Value consolidates in winners,
  dot-com style — Google/Amazon, not Lyft. If bullish perps, buy Hyperliquid and
  Lighter; don't hunt a third asset. Same logic in AI: own Intel and Nvidia,
  not everything.
- **Robinhood is a multi-bagger** — bought ~92, now ~123, still holding. Compares
  it to Interactive Brokers (Thomas Peterffy, ~$105B). Notes Robinhood chain fees
  annualize to +20–25% of bottom line but expects that to drop off; the durable
  value is tokenization, RWAs, stablecoins, and being the one financial
  institution taking crypto seriously.
- **Monetary assets:** only Bitcoin, Zcash, Monero. Zcash at $1,000, boosted by
  a DAT and broad accessibility; shielded ZEC usage is growing. Monero is the
  privacy coin actually used for privacy. Over BMNR he'd pick ZEC for upside.
- Bullish Intel to ~140 by year-end; back long Nvidia since Wednesday.
- Stated portfolio: BTC, ZEC, biotech (ARKG, XBI), Intel, Nvidia — expects
  50–100% over six months.
- Political risk: midterms could bring a short-lived dip; a Dem win raises 2028
  odds and, in his framing, eventual large-scale money printing.

## Process advice

Train the reflex of converting every headline into a market question: AMC CEO
tweets about tokenization → does this move AMC, Robinhood, a memecoin? OpenAI
ships GPT-6.0 → chip demand, SanDisk +10%. Avi keeps a physical notepad and
writes an impact hypothesis for every tweet with signal. Also: **always state
your time horizon** when naming an asset publicly.

## Caveats

- Avi is talking his book throughout (long HOOD, INTC, NVDA, BTC, ZEC) and
  promotes his FOMO referral link; the guest is the platform's co-founder.
- The 94.4% loss rate is the single most important number in the episode and
  cuts directly against the bullish memecoin framing surrounding it.
