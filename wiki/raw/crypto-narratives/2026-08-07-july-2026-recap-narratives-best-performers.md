- July 15: “Ondo Finance Debuts First-Ever Tokenized Stocks Based on DTC Tokenized Entitlements to DTC-Held Securities”. This announcement lead to a strong bounce on **$ONDO**, which pumped +35% in a week.
- **$JTO**, which has been performing very well (+250%) from April to June, has officially launched its JTX Solana trading platform on July 14. It was impossible to prevent the usual “sell the news effect”, and JTO dumped -26% in 3 weeks after the launch.
- **$GRASS** held its investor call on July 7, after going up almost +75% in a month in anticipation of it. The overall reaction has been disappointment about the lack of value accrual towards the token, despite the protocol having significant revenue. This disappointment lead to a -35% sell-the-news in one day, and -45% in 3 weeks.
- After an awful performance (-60%) in 2026 (and really since early 2024), **$LDO** finally had 2 catalysts to help it bounce in July. The first one is the upcoming launch of a ‘private AI’ product called **Wisp** and built by part of the Lido team. The value accrual mechanism for LDO is unclear but at least we know that there won’t be a second token launched. The other catalyst for LDO was the Lido Core upgrade which happened on July 27-28.
- On July 1st, **Venice** announced a $70M raise at a $1bn valuation. The coin immediately pumped +20%… but it topped there, because the raise was only for the Venice equity, which implied that we were going to have the token<>equity dual structure which has been historically so bad for the value of the tokens… So **$VVV**, which was already in a downtrend, fully retraced its pump, and then went even lower, basically from $15 to $10 in two weeks. Right when VVV started capitulating below $10, Venice announced that 5% of API credits bought on Venice would be used to buyback and burn VVV from now on, and it lead to a stronge bounce back to $15, which marked a local top and lower high.
- On July 13, someone managed to exploit the governance of the Bonk DAO to extract $20M worth of **$BONK** from the DAO. It lead to a -33% dump in a week on $BONK.
- The Korean memory company **SK Hynix** listed its ADR on Nasdaq on July 10, under the ticker **$SKHY**. SKHY is currently down from its listing price because of the AI stock overall dump, but it’s been trading roughly at a 30% premium vs the Korean shares, and that premium doesn’t really collapse. Only a very small part of the float is listed as ADR, which is why there’s a strong price gap.
- On June 30, it was announced that Nasdaq chose **Pyth** for data distribution, which lead to an immediate pump and dump, but then **$PYTH** has been strong for 2 weeks from there (+35% move up).
- **Strategy** announced their first substantial BTC sale of $200M on July 6. The proceeds were used to pay the monthly+quarterly dividends on their preferred shares. BTC dumped -2.6% on the news but immediately bottomed and bounced back up, which was a strong signal that the $**MSTR** FUD was more or less over for a while.

## $58k bottom on BTC

**$BTC** dumped to the $60k zone again in late June, and it had several breakdown attempts below $58k-$59k which all failed. It was very clear from the price action that there were buyers in this zone, and the pico-bottom got reached on a sweep of the lows below $58k precisely at the beginning of July. This was an excellent opportunity to get positioned long for a bounce, which turned out to be a +15% bounce to almost $67k on July 21st.

## Robinhood chain launch

The main narrative of July in crypto was unquestionably the launch of Robinhood chain, which is an Ethereum L2. Its official launch took place on July 1st, and it was a catalyst for several moves:

- **$UNI** had a very strong month, it was the best performing large cap in July, as it went up more than +60%. The catalysts were: the fact that it was the main DEX on Robinhood + they enabled fees on the Robinhood chain, which had significant activity after its launch + they enabled fees on Uni V4 on several chains, for the first time (it was a governance proposal, and got approved in July).

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!QHTC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F913125c4-1507-4c7a-b042-427284a69d4a_2244x1576.png)

- **$LIT** was announced as the official perp DEX of the chain, and it’s been a strong catalyst for LIT, which then pumped from $1.8 to $2.7 in a few days. At that level, LIT was roughly reaching parity in terms of annualized fees / FDV with Hyperliquid, meaning it wasn’t “underpriced” versus $HYPE anymore. $LIT had a triple top at $2.7 and then went back down to $2.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!M59h!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F710be94d-275e-4a8c-962a-ac6be4bdcf26_2244x1578.png)

- **$CASHCAT** quickly became the main memecoin on the chain, and it pumped from $8M to $220M mcap at the top. It then bleed down to $40M market cap where it found a base.
- **$ARB** pumped +30% in reaction to the high activity on the chain, because the Robinhood chain is an Arbitrum chain. It did quickly retrace this whole pump, though, which is what can generally be expected from L2 coins… (a cursed category)
- **$DYDX** had been teasing an announcement for several days, which lead to a 2x move in June, but it completely collapsed and fully retraced that move when Robinhood put the spotlight onto Lighter rather than Dydx, on July 1st. They announced a new stock spot/perp trading platform on Robinhood chain called Arcus though, but it didn’t prevent the strong sell the news effect.
- **Maple** launched syrupUSDG and syrupUSDC on Robinhood chain, their AUM grew quickly, **$SYRUP** went up +50% bottom to top in July.

## The AI and momentum sell-off in equities, & the Leopold sacrifice

The most important thing that happened in financial markets in July was the heavy sell-off in all the AI names, and especially memory, which had all been the top performers of the year. They were basically in a downtrend during the whole month, and their drawdown was very significant. It was one of the worst moves for the momentum stocks in several decades. The S&P 500 didn’t care a lot, but the Nasdaq got impact too, it went down -11% from top to bottom in July.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rqWz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F022c5688-8ff6-4d29-bea9-5943b9c1a20e_2242x1578.png)

Here are the drawdowns of the main memory names (which were all up at least 10x in a year):

- **$MU (Micron)**: -44%
- **$SNDK (Sandisk)**: -59%
- **SK Hynix**: -58%
- **Samsung**: -50%
- **Kioxia**: -66%

The main reasons for this dump were concerns about a **capex decrease** from the hyperscalers (which would imply less revenue for these memory / AI infra stocks), as well as the inevitable unwind of a momentum trade that had been going on for months with explosive performance and some people getting more and more leveraged, especially in Korea which has 2 of the largest memory companies in the world (SK Hynix and Samsung).

It also turned out that **Leopold Aschenbrenner** got in serious trouble once AI stocks started selling off. He’s a 25yo who’s leading the Situational Awareness hedge fund, which had an extremely good performance until the top of the AI stocks. With the sell-off that happened in July, and given the fact that he was levered on his bets, Leopold was forced to ‘liquidate’ his positions by selling his whole liquid book to Citadel Securities. That was announced on July 30, one day after the bottom in stocks, and it immediately lead to a huge bounce on all these AI names (memory, semis, neoclouds, etc). The sacrifice of Leopold was necessary for the market to bottom.

Hyperscalers (Mag7) and software stocks closed the month in green, as they are now often used as pair trades against the AI infra basket.

## +60% on PUMP despite unlocks

**$PUMP** went through its first major unlock wave in July but it actually marked a local bottom. The onchain distribution of tokens happened on July 14, and PUMP swept its lows on that day, but given the fact that no tokens were sent to exchanges, the market turned bullish on PUMP, and it pumped +60% from there, making it one of the top performing non scam coins in July.

## Scam coins of July

As usual, we had several crime coin moves in July:

- **$AKE** went up 35x bottom to top in 2 weeks. And it topped with a 2x liquidation candle that was ‘shortable’ if you were ready to react on short timeframes.
- **$BANK** had a similar move, it pumped almost 20x in total. It had a topping squeeze exactly at the same moment as AKE on July 27, but surprisingly, it’s been completely dying from there (-93%), whereas AKE remained stable.
- **$LAB**, which was the main scam coin in June, dumped from $20bn to $6bn FDV in early July, then bounced back to $18bn FDV… and then it completely collapsed, and literally went down more than -99% in 2 weeks.
- Another scam coin that died completely is **$DEXE**. It pumped more than 2x in July (25x from February), but then had a -96% dump.
- **$TAC** had a rather similar move as DEXE (20x in 3 months), it also dumped -94% in July…

## What else happened in July?

- **ZCash** announced in early July that the Ironwood update would take place on July 28, which would help confirm that no **$ZEC** has been minted by an attacker. The good news lead ZEC to pump +30% but it then retraced the move during the second half of July. It remains one of the strongest coins in the market.
- In July, **crude oil** rebounded sharply ($67 → $94 for $CL) as renewed U.S.–Iran tensions, strikes, and threats to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz brought the geopolitical risk premium back into the market. It then gave back a large part of the move ($94 → $74) as strikes paused and hopes for negotiations and safer Gulf shipping increased.
- The memecoin **$ANSEM** started the month of July around $160M, and it kept pumping for several days, until it reached $440M MC. This level marked the top and ANSEM fully retraced the July move, but it still ranging between $160M and $200M MC. Be aware that 60% of the supply is cornered into a single wallet held by Ansem, meaning the ‘real market cap’ topped around $200M, in line with most of the onchain runner tops during this bear market.
- ETH outperformed BTC in July, and **ETHBTC went from 0.027 to 0.03**. This doesn’t change the fact that ETHBTC is still in a downtrend since August 2025, until it manages to reclaim 0.032. In the same time, BTC outperformed altcoins massively. BTC had a significant capitulation against altcoins from early May to early June (when it got back to its $60k lows after the Strategy ‘test sale’), and it’s been trending up vs alts since then.
- **$ZAMA** (a privacy coin) has been in a very slow and steady uptrend since February 2026. $0.035 has been acting as a strong resistance for months, but ZAMA finally broke out above it on July 20. It then pumped even more 2 days after once Tushar (from Multicoin) started shilling it. ZAMA went up 2x in July.
- **$KAITO** has been suspiciously strong since its bottom in February 2026, and there has been a strong acceleration to the upside in July, with a +130% move bottom to top (and 5x in total since February). I suspect KAITO to have a heavily cornered supply, which would explain this price action. On July 23, Kaito announced a data agreement with X, but it didn’t top there, and kept going up afterwards.
- **EtherFi** has been teasing an important announcement since early July. The announcement was supposed to take place during their July 30 investor call, but it got delayed to August 13. **$ETHFI** went up +50% bottom to top in July.
- Despite not moving up a lot, **$ENA** has been quite strong on a relative basis for an altcoin, it pumped +30% in three weeks. No real catalyst, but ENA is quite beaten down so it was a mechanical bounce from its $0.07 double bottom.
- **$ADA**, which had an awful -40% performance in June, had a mean reversion bounce in early July, it went up +40% in 3 days, then retraced 2/3 of its pump before moving up again.
- **$SYN**, which had a meteoric 25x rise in June, has spent the whole month of July bleeding, it went down -86% in a month.
- **$WLFI** has more or less been in zombie mode for weeks if not months, with a very slow bleeding an no attention. On July 23, it had a -10% dump immediately followed by a +30% bounce, which was a great short opportunity because the move got retraced in just a few days…
