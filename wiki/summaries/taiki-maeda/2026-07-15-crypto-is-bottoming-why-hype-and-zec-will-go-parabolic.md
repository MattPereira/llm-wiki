# Crypto is Bottoming. Why HYPE and ZEC will go PARABOLIC

**Taiki Maeda** · 2026-07-15 · 32:26 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wTmGFHhUUY)

## Thesis

Maeda believes crypto is already in a bottoming process, so investors should
slowly accumulate a few likely cycle winners rather than try to call the exact
low. His portfolio expresses two bets: HYPE/LIT on non-crypto perpetuals becoming
a large retail market, and ZEC on privacy, quantum resistance, and Bitcoin's
Michael Saylor overhang becoming more important narratives.

## Market-cycle view

- He dates peak euphoria to July–August 2025, when BTC first reached ~$125K, ETH
  reached ~$5K, and digital-asset-treasury mania peaked—not the weak October BTC
  high near $126K. On that clock, crypto has already endured roughly a year of
  bear-market attrition.
- He does not reject a Q4 low or the four-year cycle. His narrower claim: as in
  June 2022, accumulating strong assets during the *bottoming process* can work
  even if prices later make new lows.
- He claims no edge in short-term BTC timing. His proposed edge is a long horizon,
  concentrated research, and conviction sufficient to hold for 6–18 months.
- Many investors are waiting to buy in Q4; he argues that crowded expectation can
  pull buying forward and make waiting for the exact low costly.

## HYPE, LIT, and the perp-DEX thesis

The durable bull case is not more crypto speculation. It is perpetual futures
expanding into real-world assets—gold, oil, and equities—and taking retail share
from options as a simpler leveraged directional product. Since Maeda expects most
altcoins to approach zero, a crypto-only perp venue eventually fights over a
shrinking market.

- Hyperliquid's HIPP-3 markets position it as the RWA-perp leader; Lighter's
  Robinhood relationship could supply distribution. Maeda owns both rather than
  treating the category as winner-take-all.
- He cites token buybacks equal to roughly **3.4% of HYPE** and **6.3% of LIT**
  circulating supply year-to-date as current support, but treats continued RWA
  open-interest growth as the more important signal.
- He says HYPE/LIT weakness caused only by BTC or ETH would be a buying
  opportunity because their intended end market is not crypto-native demand.
- He also farms Variational, citing ~$1.2–1.3B open interest and roughly 25% in
  traditional-asset markets. He prefers organic trades or shorts against spot
  holdings, turning necessary hedges into airdrop exposure.
- His illustrative Variational valuation—25% airdrop at $1B implying ~$27 per
  point, possibly launching at $2–3B—is speculative and depends on assumptions he
  does not substantiate.

## Why he rebought ZEC

Maeda sold his entire ZEC position near the low after a suspected Orchard shielded
pool vulnerability helped drive a ~60% decline. He later rebought because price
recovery matched his precommitted condition: surviving a potentially fatal shock
would make Zcash more antifragile and could restart a reflexive narrative cycle.
He emphasizes ignoring the pain of the prior loss and evaluating the asset from
its current forward odds.

His technical catalyst is the planned **Ironwood** shielded pool, which he says
will add quantum resistance and formal verification intended to prevent
undetectable counterfeiting bugs. His own recap concedes important uncertainty:
Orchard may have been vulnerable, exploitation is unknown, there is no public
evidence of exploitation, and no upgrade can guarantee the absence of undiscovered
bugs. Privacy itself makes supply-integrity verification harder.

## ZEC versus BTC

Maeda frames ZEC as a complement to BTC—crypto's silver to its gold—not a claim
that ZEC is currently the superior monetary asset.

- He expects privacy and quantum risk to matter more over time. He also worries
  Bitcoin developers lack urgency on quantum migration.
- He sees Michael Saylor as execution and narrative risk: after years as a major
  buyer, Saylor becoming a recurring seller could pressure Bitcoin and makes its
  success feel increasingly tied to one person.
- These concerns may cause BTC allocators to diversify at the margin—for example,
  90% BTC / 10% ZEC rather than 100% BTC—without abandoning Bitcoin.
- ZEC remains below 1% of BTC's market capitalization, in his telling. Crossing
  that ratio near prior ZEC resistance around $650–700 could combine relative and
  dollar-price breakouts, reinforcing attention, inflows, and the thesis itself.
- He sold some BTC to buy ZEC but says the position is now large enough and he
  will not rotate more. His upside cases of 2–10% of BTC's market cap are
  scenarios, not forecasts grounded in adoption data.

## Portfolio/process lessons

- Own and stake HYPE/LIT for 6–12 months; farm additional perp venues to create
  exposure to possible future winners.
- Keep cash both as dry powder and a psychological buffer. He believes being
  oversized in ZEC made the prior drawdown and sale more emotional.
- Concentrate only where conviction supports a long holding period, but retain
  enough cash to survive being wrong and exploit later dips or narratives.
- Treat losses as sunk: revising a thesis and rebuying higher can be rational if
  new evidence improves the forward setup.

## Caveats

- This is a disclosed personal-position book talk: Maeda is long BTC, ZEC, HYPE,
  and LIT, farms Variational, promotes referral links, and sells a premium Discord.
- Core evidence is narrative and price action. RWA-perp adoption, ZEC inflows,
  quantum concern, upgrade safety, buyback figures, and valuation claims are not
  independently verified in the video.
- The bottom call is deliberately unfalsifiable in the short run: he allows new
  Q4 lows while describing the broader period as a bottoming process.
- Incentives can inflate perp activity, while the privacy properties central to
  Zcash also make the alleged non-exploitation difficult to prove.
